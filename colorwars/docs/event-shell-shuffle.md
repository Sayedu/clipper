# Shell Shuffle brief

Read docs/phase-1.md and docs/phase-2.md first. Add a new event, shell_shuffle, on the same render, audio, league and packaging pipeline. Save this brief to docs/event-shell-shuffle.md.

## THE GAME
- 4 identical dark cups on a table, each hiding one team ball. Show the balls for 2 s, then drop the cups over them.
- Round 1: 10 slow swaps. Round 2: 16 faster swaps. Round 3: 24 swaps with trick moves: double swaps, fake swaps that start and return, an orbit where all four circle the center, and a lift tease.
- Swaps travel along arcs with slight scale changes so one cup passes in front. Ease in and out, never teleport.
- After rounds 1 and 2, lift all cups for a quick reveal with GOT IT?
- After round 3 the cups land on podium spots labeled 1ST, 2ND, 3RD, 4TH. Freeze 3 s with the cups numbered and WHERE IS YOUR TEAM? COMMENT THE CUP.
- Reveal from 4th up to 1st with a drumroll; 1st gets the win sting and confetti. Placements and points come from the podium spots.
- All motion seeded and deterministic. Total video 61 to 70 s including the standings outro.

## SOUND
Soft swish for cup slides, a click per swap, a pop per reveal, plus the drumroll and win sting from phase 1.

## MINING
Reward runs where the crown team's cup moves the most in round 3 and where 1st place is not the crown team. Reject runs where any cup sits still for all of round 3.
